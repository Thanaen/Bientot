# Code of conduct

Bientôt follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, and keep discussions about
the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people
who do not respect these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or
contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Thanaen).
Reports are handled privately.
